Music, without the noise.

Lunara is a premium Android music player with a sign-in-free, account-free listening experience. A full catalogue, synced lyrics, and a Material You interface that colours itself to whatever you play.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A complete listening experience, built to feel instant and stay out of your way.

Full catalogue

Browse pages, artists, albums, playlists and a live home feed that keeps discovering with you.

Material You

The interface colours itself from the album art — every song gets its own mood.

Synced lyrics

Time-synced lyrics from multiple providers, so the words land on the beat.

Offline & downloads

Save songs and playlists for the subway, the flight, anywhere the signal drops.

No account, ever

Lunara never asks you to sign in and never restores a Google cookie. Just listen.

Widgets & shortcuts

Home-screen widgets, quick settings and a persistent player that follows you around.

Share a song like a poster.

Every song, album, playlist and artist can be shared as a story-sized picture — the artwork, the title, the artist, and a link that opens right here in Lunara. Send it to Instagram, WhatsApp, anywhere.

Get Lunara

Three steps and you're listening.

1

Download the APK

Grab the latest release from GitHub. Sideload it like any other Android app.

2

Open a shared link

Any Lunara link opens straight in the app — no app store in the way.

3

Press play

No sign-in, no setup. Your library, queue and lyrics are ready when you are.
