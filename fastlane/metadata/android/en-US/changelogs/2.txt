Lunara 1.0.1 - initial release.

A clean, focused music player for Android.

Music playback: play, pause, skip and seek with background playback, notification controls and lock screen controls.

Library management: browse by songs, albums, artists and playlists, create custom playlists, and search your collection.

Audio quality: built-in equalizer with presets, adjustable playback speed, and a sleep timer.

Interface: clean modern design with light and dark theme support.

Requires Android 8.0 or higher.
