# Sharing from Lunara

Every song, album, playlist and artist has a share button. It builds a story-sized picture —
the artwork, the title, the artist — and a link like

```
https://lunara.ankitak.com.np/song/<id>
```

Pasted into WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram or iMessage, the link shows the real cover, title and
artist as its preview. Tapped on a phone with Lunara installed, it opens straight in the app;
without the app, it lands on the Lunara site so the recipient can get it.
